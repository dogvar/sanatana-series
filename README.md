## What is this?

Sanatana Series is a free, ad-free collection of guides to Hindu festivals, sacred texts and dharmic traditions — written in plain English and Hindi for diaspora families, second-generation Hindus, and anyone curious about the tradition.

Every guide covers:
- The story and meaning behind the festival
- Puja vidhi (how to observe at home)
- Key mantras with Devanagari, Roman and meaning
- Modern relevance — why it matters today
- Hindi version (हिन्दी) available for most guides
