## Narrative evidence

- [Bhagavata Purana 8.8](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/8/): Lakshmi and Dhanvantari in the churning narrative. The section is a devotional account, not a date for a historical event.
- [Bhagavata 10.59](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/59/): Krishna's defeat of Naraka; distinguish this text from Satyabhama-centred regional variants.
- [Bhagavata 10.24](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/24/) and [10.25](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/25/): Govardhan worship and shelter from the storm.
- [Bhagavata 8.22](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/22/): Bali's promise, surrender and protection in Sutala.
- [Princeton Hindu Life](https://diwali.princeton.edu/about/about-diwali/), [NALIS](https://www.nalis.gov.tt/resources/tt-content-guide/divali/): festival and regional context. Rama's lamp-filled welcome is presented as festival tradition, not as an invented Valmiki verse.
- [Panchang Guru](https://www.panchangguru.com/katha/dhanteras-katha): King Hima household folklore. No definitive scriptural verse attribution is claimed.
