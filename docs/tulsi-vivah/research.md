## Ritual and setting

- [ISKCON Navi Mumbai](https://iskcon-navimumbai.org/event_detail/Tulsi%20Vivah%20with%20Lord%20Krishna%20) describes wedding forms and a Shaligram procession around Tulsi. [ISKCON Raichur](https://iskconraichur.com/tulsi-shaligram-vivah/) supplies reported Braj, Marathi and Telugu examples. The guide does not generalize one temple’s dates or ritual to all Vaishnavas.
- The [Mumbai region’s Deulwadi heritage study](https://www.mmrhcs.org.in/images/documents/projects/precinct-studies/deulwadi_precinct/stage-2.pdf) documents decorating the plant with sugarcane, mango leaves and flowers. The [Goa Gazette](https://goaprintingpress.gov.in/downloads/2526/2526-2-SIII-OG.pdf) supplies a separate Dwadashi temple listing. [India Temple Association](https://www.indiatemple.org/pdf/satsangsandesh/1762115804November%202025%20Satsang%20FINAL.pdf) announced the historical New Jersey diaspora example. The regional selection follows the available evidence.
- Drik Panchang’s [Chaturmasa page](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/months/chaturmasa/hindu-calendar-chaturmasa.html?year=2026) and [Chaturmasya Vidhan](https://www.drikpanchang.com/ekadashis/chaturmasya-vidhan/ekadashi-chaturmasya-vidhan.html) describe traditional reckoning and the resumption of auspicious rites. The guide presents that association as a custom.
- [Holly Walters’s fieldwork](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/shaligram-pilgrimage-in-the-nepal-himalayas/living-fossils-impressions-of-a-once-and-future-world/54461A44B0B115EF8785A73CCD7D8117) documents the natural fossil forms, Kali Gandaki provenance and transregional worship of Shaligrams. The close-up is illustrative and does not classify or authenticate a sacred stone.
- [Kew’s Plants of the World Online](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A453130-1) supplies the accepted botanical name and native range. The [Siddha Pharmacopoeia of India](https://www.pcimh.gov.in/showfile.php?arch=1&lang=1&level=1&lid=608&ls_id=797&sh_arch=1) documents traditional recognition. No clinical claim or treatment recommendation is made.
