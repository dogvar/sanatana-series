# Chhath Puja 2026 · Review and QA

The draft contains complete English and Hindi editions, eight corresponding illustrations, reciprocal language links, metadata, BlogPosting structured data, and a shared social image. The homepage card covers 13–16 November. Both languages are linked from the existing 15 November calendar entry and the corresponding Diwali guide.

## Review results

- The festival validator checks the four observance dates and their order in both languages, local panchang guidance, matching anchors, image and link references, metadata alternates, and the homepage and calendar links.
- The existing-guide preservation check passed. Diwali has one additional related link in each language.
- Chrome browser QA passed at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels in both languages. Checks covered layout overflow, font loading and Devanagari rendering, keyboard skip and contents navigation, language switching, reduced motion, local resource loading, all eight images and intrinsic sizes, and the card and calendar integration. The article and table of contents remain readable with JavaScript disabled. [Browser report](browser-qa.json)
- Key text color pairs in the hero, day cards, timing panel and horizon panel were at least 7.29:1. Desktop and mobile captures were visually reviewed. Browser coverage was Chrome; no screen-reader study was run.
- The date evidence, sources, Amsterdam query limitation and artwork decisions are in the [research notes](research.md).
