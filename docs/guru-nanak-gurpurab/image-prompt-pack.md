# Guru Nanak Dev Ji: image prompt pack

Thirteen ready-to-paste prompts: 12 guide illustrations and 1 social preview image.

## How to use this pack

1. Generate one image at a time, in **landscape 3:2**. Image 13 is **wide, about 1.91:1**.
2. Paste the finished image into the chat and say which number it is, for example "Image 5".
3. I will inspect it, optimise it, place it in both editions and write its English and Hindi alt text.

**The Guru's face:** these prompts follow the respectful default of not showing Guru Nanak Dev Ji's face, because many Sikhs object to depicting the Gurus. He appears only from behind or at a distance in Images 1, 3, 5 and 6.

**Reject an image if it has:** any writing or fake Gurmukhi, a visible face for the Guru, a halo, a Hindu or Islamic symbol, temple architecture, modern objects, distorted hands, or a Khanda or Khalsa dress in Images 1 to 8.
