# Guru Nanak Dev Ji Gurpurab: image briefs

**Status: 8 of 12 images placed** (Plates 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11 and 12), plus the social image. Plates 1, 7, 8 and 9 need regeneration. See `artwork.json` and `image-prompt-pack.md`. Remaining slots show labelled "Illustration pending" panels.

## Decide before generating

Many Sikhs and Sikh institutions object to depicting the Gurus, particularly in film and animation. The SGPC opposed the film *Nanak Shah Fakir* on this ground. Plates 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8 and any future video need an owner decision. The recommended approach is to avoid showing the Guru's face: use a figure from behind or at a distance, or tell the scene through hands, landscape and objects.

## Shared direction

- **Style:** refined heritage painting informed by Punjab's Pahari-Sikh and Lahore manuscript traditions. Natural pigments, restrained detail. No comic-book, anime, glossy 3D or poster styling.
- **Palette:** ivory, deep indigo, muted saffron, warm gold.
- **Never include:** generated text or fake Gurmukhi, halos added by default, Hindu temple architecture, deities, Om, lotus thrones, swastika, tilak, or Islamic or Hindu symbols used as decoration.
- **Period check (plates 2–8):** late fifteenth to early sixteenth century Punjab. No Khalsa-era dress or the Khanda, which come later. No modern objects.
- **Output:** 1440×960 WebP in `images/guru-nanak-gurpurab/`, plus a 1200×630 `social.jpg`. Record prompt, tool and review in an `artwork.json`. Captions should say "AI-generated illustration" where applicable.
