Devotional Songs of Hindu Tradition

Aarti Sangrah

आरती संग्रह

Sacred hymns — Devanagari · Roman · English meaning

Aarti (आरती) — the ritual of light — is the most universal act of Hindu devotion.

The word comes from Sanskrit Aratrika (आरात्रिक) — "that which removes darkness." An aarti is performed by waving a flame before the deity while singing a hymn — the light symbolising divine consciousness dispelling the darkness of ignorance. Aarti is sung at the end of every puja, at sunrise and sunset in temples, and at every festival. Even if you know nothing else about Hinduism, if you know the aarti, you can participate in any worship anywhere in India.

1
Lord Vishnu · Universal

Om Jai Jagdish Hare

ॐ जय जगदीश हरे — "OM, glory to the Lord of the Universe"
Composed by Pundit Shardha Ram Phillauri (1863). The most universally sung aarti in India — performed at virtually every puja of every deity. Many Hindus believe meditating on this aarti gives the blessings of all gods simultaneously. It is dedicated to Lord Vishnu (Jagdish = Lord of the Universe) but sung for all occasions.
ॐ जय जगदीश हरे, स्वामी जय जगदीश हरे।
भक्त जनों के संकट, दास जनों के संकट, क्षण में दूर करे॥
Om Jai Jagdish Hare, Swami Jai Jagdish Hare
Bhakt Jano Ke Sankat, Daas Jano Ke Sankat, Kshan Mein Door Kare
OM, glory to the Lord of the Universe. In an instant He removes all afflictions of His devotees and servants.
जो ध्यावे फल पावे, दुख बिनसे मन का।
सुख सम्पति घर आवे, कष्ट मिटे तन का॥
Jo Dhyave Phal Pave, Dukh Binse Man Ka
Sukh Sampati Ghar Aave, Kasht Mite Tan Ka
Whoever meditates on Him receives the fruit — the sorrow of the mind vanishes. Happiness and prosperity come home; the suffering of the body is relieved.
मात पिता तुम मेरे, शरण गहूं किसकी।
तुम बिन और न दूजा, आस करूं जिसकी॥
Maat Pita Tum Mere, Sharan Gahun Kiskee
Tum Bin Aur Na Dooja, Aas Karun Jiskee
You are my mother and father — whose shelter shall I seek? Without You there is no other in whom I place my hope.
तुम पूरन परमात्मा, तुम अन्तर्यामी।
पारब्रह्म परमेश्वर, तुम सबके स्वामी॥
Tum Puran Parmatma, Tum Antaryami
Par-Brahm Parmeshwar, Tum Sabke Swami
You are the complete Supreme Soul, the inner witness of all. You are the Absolute Brahman, the Supreme Lord, master of all creation.
तुम करुणा के सागर, तुम पालनकर्ता।
मैं मूरख खल कामी, कृपा करो भर्ता॥
Tum Karuna Ke Saagar, Tum Palankarta
Main Moorakh Khal Kami, Kripa Karo Bharta
You are the ocean of compassion, the sustainer of all. I am foolish, corrupt and full of desire — have mercy on me, O Lord.
तुम हो एक अगोचर, सबके प्राणपति।
किस विधि मिलूं दयामय, तुमको मैं कुमति॥
Tum Ho Ek Agochar, Sabke Pranpati
Kis Vidhi Milun Dayamay, Tumko Main Kumati
You are the one invisible, the lord of the life of all. How can I, of poor intellect, find you, O compassionate one?
दीनबंधु दुखहर्ता, ठाकुर तुम मेरे।
अपने हाथ उठाओ, अपनी शरण लगाओ, द्वार खड़ा तेरे॥
Deenbandhu Dukhaharta, Thakur Tum Mere
Apne Haath Uthaao, Apni Sharan Lagaao, Dwar Khada Tere
Friend of the humble, destroyer of sorrow — You are my Lord. Lift up Your hands, take me under Your shelter — I stand at Your door.
विषय विकार मिटाओ, पाप हरो देवा।
श्रद्धा भक्ति बढ़ाओ, श्रद्धा प्रेम बढ़ाओ, संतन की सेवा॥
Vishay Vikaar Mitaao, Paap Haro Deva
Shradha Bhakti Badhaao, Shradha Prem Badhaao, Santan Ki Seva
Remove worldly attachments and corruption, destroy sin, O Divine One. Increase my faith and devotion, increase love and faith — let me serve Your saints.

Goddess Durga · Navratri · All Shakti festivals

Jai Ambe Gauri

जय अम्बे गौरी — "Glory to the Golden-Hued Mother Ambika"
The primary aarti of Goddess Durga, sung during Navratri and all Devi festivals. Ambe refers to the primordial mother (Amba = mother); Gauri refers to her fair/golden complexion as Parvati. This aarti praises all nine forms of the Goddess. It is traditionally sung at the end of the evening prayer throughout the nine nights of Navratri.
जय अम्बे गौरी, मैया जय श्यामा गौरी।
तुमको निशदिन ध्यावत, हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी॥
Jai Ambe Gauri, Maiya Jai Shyama Gauri
Tumko Nishdin Dhavat, Hari Brahma Shivari
Glory to Ambe Gauri! Mother, glory to Shyama Gauri! Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on You day and night without rest.
माँग सिन्दूर विराजत, टीको मृगमद को।
उज्ज्वल से दो नैना, चन्द्रवदन नीको॥
Maang Sindoor Virajat, Teeko Mrigmad Ko
Ujjawal Se Do Naina, Chandravadan Neeko
Sindoor adorns her parting, a tilak of musk-deer fragrance on her brow. Her two eyes are radiant, her moon-like face is beautiful.
कनक समान कलेवर, रक्ताम्बर राजे।
रक्त पुष्प गल माला, कण्ठन पर साजे॥
Kanak Samaan Kalevar, Raktaambar Raaje
Rakt Pushp Gal Maala, Kanthan Par Saaje
Her body is golden like burnished gold, her red garment shines magnificently. A garland of red flowers adorns her throat, beautifully decorated.
केहरि वाहन राजत, खड़ग खप्पर धारी।
सुर-नर-मुनिजन सेवत, तिनके दुखहारी॥
Kehari Vahan Raajat, Khadag Khappar Dhari
Sur-Nar-Munijan Sevat, Tinke Dukhhari
She sits magnificently on her lion, bearing sword and skull-cup. Gods, humans and sages serve her — she who destroys their suffering.
तुम ही जग की माता, तुम ही हो भर्ता।
भक्तन के दुख हर्ता, सुख सम्पति कर्ता॥
Tum Hi Jag Ki Maata, Tum Hi Ho Bharta
Bhaktan Ke Dukh Harta, Sukh Sampati Karta
You alone are the mother of the world, You alone are its sustainer. You remove the sufferings of devotees and bestow happiness and prosperity.
अम्बे माँ की आरती जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख सम्पति पावे॥
Ambe Maa Ki Aarti Jo Koi Nar Gaave
Kahat Shivananand Swami, Sukh Sampati Paave
Whoever sings this aarti of Mother Ambe — says Swami Shivananda — shall attain happiness and prosperity.

Lord Ganesha · All beginnings · Ganesh Chaturthi

Jai Ganesh Jai Ganesh Deva

जय गणेश देवा — "Glory to Lord Ganesha"
The most popular aarti of Lord Ganesha — the remover of obstacles and lord of all beginnings. Sung before every puja, every important undertaking, every exam, every journey. Ganesha is always worshipped first before any other deity. His aarti sets the tone for every ceremony. The mother in this aarti refers to Goddess Parvati; the father is Lord Shiva.
जय गणेश जय गणेश जय गणेश देवा।
माता जाकी पार्वती पिता महादेवा॥
Jai Ganesh Jai Ganesh Jai Ganesh Deva
Maata Jaaki Parvati Pita Mahadeva
Glory to Ganesha! Glory to Ganesha! Glory to Lord Ganesha! Whose mother is Parvati and father is Mahadeva (Shiva).
एकदन्त दयावन्त, चार भुजाधारी।
माथे सिन्दूर सोहे, मूसे की सवारी॥
Ekdant Dayavant, Chaar Bhujaadhaari
Maathe Sindoor Sohe, Moose Ki Savari
Single-tusked, compassionate, bearing four arms. Sindoor adorns his forehead; the mouse is his vehicle.
पान चढे, फूल चढे, और चढे मेवा।
लड्डुअन का भोग लगे, संत करें सेवा॥
Paan Chadhe, Phool Chadhe, Aur Chadhe Meva
Ladduan Ka Bhog Lage, Sant Karen Seva
Pan is offered, flowers are offered, and dry fruits too. An offering of laddoos is placed, and saints perform His service.
अंधन को आँख देत, कोढ़िन को काया।
बाँझन को पुत्र देत, निर्धन को माया॥
Andhan Ko Aankh Det, Kodin Ko Kaya
Baanjhan Ko Putr Det, Nirdhan Ko Maya
He gives sight to the blind, a healthy body to the diseased. He gives children to the childless, and wealth to the poor.
दीनन की लाज रखो, शम्भु सुतकारी।
कामना को पूर्ण करो, जाऊं बलिहारी॥
Deenan Ki Laaj Rakho, Shambhu Sutkari
Kamna Ko Poorn Karo, Jaun Balihaaree
Protect the honour of the humble, O son of Shambhu. Fulfil all desires — I sacrifice myself to You in devotion.

Lord Hanuman · Tuesdays & Saturdays · Hanuman Jayanti

Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki

आरती कीजे हनुमान लला की — "Perform the aarti of young Hanuman"
The primary aarti of Lord Hanuman — sung after the Hanuman Chalisa, every Tuesday and Saturday (his sacred days), and on Hanuman Jayanti. This aarti celebrates Hanuman's extraordinary qualities: his strength, his devotion to Rama, his role in the Lanka War, and his status as the divine remover of all obstacles and negative forces.
आरती कीजे हनुमान लला की।
दुष्ट दलन रघुनाथ कला की॥
Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki
Dusht Dalan Raghunath Kala Ki
Perform the aarti of young Hanuman — He who destroys evil and embodies the glory of Lord Raghunath (Rama).
जाके बल से गिरिवर काँपे।
रोग दोष जाके निकट न झाँपे॥
Jake Bal Se Girivar Kaanpe
Rog Dosh Jake Nikat Na Jhaanpe
By whose strength the greatest mountains tremble. Disease and defects dare not come near Him.
अंजनि पुत्र महा बलदाई।
संतन के प्रभु सदा सहाई॥
Anjani Putr Maha Baldai
Santan Ke Prabhu Sada Sahai
Son of Anjani, the greatly powerful one. Lord of the saints, always their helper and support.
दे बीड़ा रघुनाथ पठाए।
लंका जारि सिया सुधि लाए॥
De Beera Raghunath Pathaye
Lanka Jaari Siya Sudhi Laaye
Sent on a mission by Raghunath (Rama). He burned Lanka and brought back news of Sita.
लंका विध्वंस कीन्ह रघुराई।
तुलसीदास प्रभु कीरति गाई॥
Lanka Vidhwans Keen Raghurai
Tulsidas Prabhu Keerti Gaai
Lanka was destroyed under the banner of Raghurai (Rama). Tulsidas has sung the glory of this great Lord.

Lord Shiva · Mondays · Maha Shivaratri · Shravan month

Om Jai Shiv Omkara

ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा — "OM, glory to Shiva, the source of OM"
The primary aarti of Lord Shiva — the destroyer and transformer in the Hindu Trinity. Sung on Mondays (Shiva's day), during Maha Shivaratri, and throughout the sacred month of Shravan. Shiva is described here in all his forms: as the cosmic dancer, as the one who wears the Ganga in his matted locks, as the supreme renunciant who holds all siddhi powers.
ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा, स्वामी जय शिव ओंकारा।
ब्रह्मा विष्णु सदाशिव, अर्धांगी धारा॥
Om Jai Shiv Omkara, Swami Jai Shiv Omkara
Brahma Vishnu Sadashiv, Ardhaangi Dhara
OM, glory to Shiva the source of OM! Brahma, Vishnu and Sadashiva — three aspects of the One held in the half-body form (Ardhanarishvara).
एकानन चतुरानन पञ्चानन राजे।
हंसासन गरुड़ासन वृषवाहन साजे॥
Ekaanan Chaturaanan Panchanan Raaje
Hansaasan Garudaasan Vrushvaahan Saaje
One-faced, four-faced, five-faced — all faces shine gloriously. Riding the swan (Brahma), the eagle (Vishnu), the bull (Shiva) — all united.
दो भुज चार चतुर्भुज दसभुज अति सोहे।
तीनों रूप निरखते त्रिभुवन जन मोहे॥
Do Bhuj Chaar Chaturbhuj Dasbhuj Ati Sohe
Teenon Roop Nirakhte Tribhuvan Jan Mohe
Two-armed, four-armed, ten-armed — all radiantly beautiful. Beholding these three forms, all the people of the three worlds are enchanted.
अक्षमाला वनमाला मुण्डमाला धारी।
चन्दन मृगमद सोहे भाले शशि धारी॥
Akshamala Vanamala Mundamala Dhari
Chandan Mrigmad Sohe Bhaale Shashi Dhari
He wears the rosary of beads, garland of forest flowers, and garland of skulls. Sandalwood and musk-deer fragrance adorn him; a crescent moon rests on his forehead.
ब्रह्माण्डों के ईश्वर, नन्दिवाहन राजे।
काशी में सदा बसे, दीनों के दुख भाजे॥
Brahmaandon Ke Ishwar, Nandivahan Raaje
Kaashi Mein Sada Base, Deenon Ke Dukh Bhaaje
Lord of all universes, He rides magnificently on Nandi the bull. He dwells forever in Kashi (Varanasi), removing the sorrows of the humble.

Lord Rama · Ram Navami · Daily Ram puja

Aarti Shri Ramayana Ji Ki

आरती श्री रामायण जी की — Aarti of the sacred Ramayana
This unique aarti is not just to Lord Rama — it is performed to the sacred text of the Ramayana itself. In Hindu tradition, the scripture is treated as a living divine presence. When the Ramayana is read in a home or temple, this aarti concludes the reading. It praises the Ramayana for containing the complete wisdom of dharma, brahmavidya (knowledge of the Absolute) and devotion.
आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीर्ति कलित ललित सिय पी की॥
Aarti Shri Ramayan Ji Ki, Keerti Kalit Lalit Siya Pi Ki
The aarti of the sacred Ramayana — which celebrates the beautiful and eternal glory of Siya (Sita) and her beloved (Rama).
गावत ब्रह्मादिक मुनि नारद, बाल्मीक बिगियान बखानी।
शुक सनकादिक शेष अरु शारद, बरनि पवन सुत कीरति मानी॥
Gaavat Brahmaadik Muni Narad, Valmiki Bigiyaan Bakhaani
Shuk Sankaadik Shesh Aru Shard, Barni Pavan Sut Keerti Maani
Brahma, sage Narada and Valmiki sing it, proclaiming its spiritual wisdom. Shuka, Sanaka, Shesha and Sharada (Saraswati) have all honoured the glory of the son of the wind (Hanuman).
गावत सेवक भक्त मुरारी, पावत सुख सम्पति नर नारी।
सुर मुनि भावत पूर्ण हितकारी, अनुज सहित मिलि कीरति पावत॥
Gaavat Sevak Bhakt Muraari, Paavat Sukh Sampati Nar Naari
Sur Muni Bhaavat Poorn Hitkaari, Anuj Sahit Mil Keerti Paavat
Devotees and servants who sing it receive happiness and prosperity, men and women both. Gods and sages find it completely beneficial — and together with brothers they attain glory.

Goddess Saraswati · Vasant Panchami · Before study

Jai Saraswati Mata

जय सरस्वती माता — "Glory to Mother Saraswati"
The aarti of Goddess Saraswati — deity of knowledge, music, arts, wisdom and learning. Sung on Vasant Panchami (her birthday in spring), on the first day of a school term, before examinations, before any creative or intellectual endeavour. Saraswati holds the veena (musical instrument), a book (Vedas), and a rosary, seated on a white lotus. Especially relevant for students.
जय सरस्वती माता, मैया जय सरस्वती माता।
सद्गुण वैभव शालिनी, त्रिभुवन विख्याता॥
Jai Saraswati Mata, Maiya Jai Saraswati Mata
Sadgun Vaibhav Shalini, Tribhuvan Vikhyata
Glory to Mother Saraswati! Endowed with good virtues and splendour, renowned throughout all three worlds.
चन्द्रवदनी पद्मासिनी, द्युति मंगलकारी।
सोहे शुभ हंस सवारी, अतुल तेजधारी॥
Chandravadani Padmaasini, Dyuti Mangalkari
Sohe Shubh Hans Savari, Atul Tejdhari
Moon-faced, seated on a lotus, radiant and auspicious. She shines on her sacred swan, bearing incomparable brilliance.
बाएं कर में वीणा, दाएं कर माला।
शीश मुकुट मणि सोहे, गल मोतियन माला॥
Baaye Kar Mein Veena, Daaye Kar Mala
Sheesh Mukut Mani Sohe, Gal Motiyan Mala
In her left hand a veena (lute), in her right a rosary. A jewelled crown adorns her head; a garland of pearls adorns her neck.
जो चित लाय भजे, तुमको भव से तारे।
मातु पिता भाई बन्धु, यही सब हमारे॥
Jo Chit Laay Bhaje, Tumko Bhav Se Taare
Matu Pita Bhai Bandhu, Yahi Sab Hamare
Whoever devotedly worships You is carried across the ocean of existence. Mother, father, brother, friend — You are all of these to us.

Goddess Lakshmi · Diwali · Friday puja · Prosperity

Jai Lakshmi Mata

जय लक्ष्मी माता — "Glory to Mother Lakshmi"
The primary aarti of Goddess Lakshmi — goddess of wealth, prosperity, beauty, grace and abundance. Sung every Friday (her sacred day), every Diwali evening, and during all Lakshmi pujas. Lakshmi is also Vishnu's consort — where she is present, Vishnu resides too. The aarti praises her four forms: Mahakali, Mahasaraswati, Mahalakshmi and Maha Durga.
जय लक्ष्मी माता, मैया जय लक्ष्मी माता।
तुमको निशदिन सेवत, हर विष्णु विधाता॥
Jai Lakshmi Mata, Maiya Jai Lakshmi Mata
Tumko Nishdin Sevat, Har Vishnu Vidhata
Glory to Mother Lakshmi! Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma (Vidhata) serve You day and night without rest.
उमा रमा ब्रह्माणी, तुम ही जग माता।
सूर्य चन्द्रमा ध्यावत, नारद ऋषि गाता॥
Uma Rama Brahmaani, Tum Hi Jag Mata
Surya Chandrama Dhyavat, Narad Rishi Gata
Uma (Parvati), Rama (Lakshmi), Brahmani — You alone are the mother of the world. The Sun and Moon meditate on You; sage Narada sings Your praises.
दुर्गा रूप निरंजनी, सुख सम्पति दाता।
जो कोई तुमको ध्यावत, ऋद्धि सिद्धि पाता॥
Durga Roop Niranjani, Sukh Sampati Data
Jo Koi Tumko Dhyavat, Riddhi Siddhi Pata
In the form of Durga, the pure and stainless one — giver of happiness and prosperity. Whoever meditates on You attains Riddhi (wealth) and Siddhi (accomplishment).
तुम पाताल निवासिनी, तुम ही शुभदाता।
कर्म प्रभाव प्रकाशिनी, भवनिधि त्राता॥
Tum Pataal Nivasini, Tum Hi Shubhdata
Karm Prabhav Prakashini, Bhavnidhi Trata
You dwell in the netherworld, You alone bestow auspiciousness. You illuminate the effects of karma, saving us from the ocean of worldly existence.
जिस घर में तुम रहती, सब सद्गुण आता।
सब सम्भव हो जाता, मन नहिं घबराता॥
Jis Ghar Mein Tum Rahati, Sab Sadgun Aata
Sab Sambhav Ho Jaata, Man Nahin Ghabrata
In whatever home You dwell, all good qualities come. Everything becomes possible; the mind does not fear.

How to Perform Aarti

  1. Light the lamp: Use a small brass or clay lamp with ghee or oil and a cotton wick. If unavailable, a simple candle works. The flame represents divine consciousness.
  2. Face the deity: Stand or sit before the idol or image. Ring a bell (if available) to announce your presence to the divine.
  3. Move the lamp in circles: Wave the lamp in slow, clockwise circles before the deity — usually 7 circles. This act is itself the aarti.
  4. Sing the aarti: Sing or recite the appropriate aarti — alone or with family. Even reading without singing is meritorious.
  5. Receive the blessing: Pass the flame to all present — each person briefly cups their hands over it and brings them to their eyes and forehead. This is receiving the divine light.
