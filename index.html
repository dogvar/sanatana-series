About This Series

The Sanatana Series is a personal project to make Hindu festivals, epics and philosophy accessible to every generation — whether you grew up in India or were born in Rotterdam. Each guide is written to be read by anyone: believer, curious learner, or someone sharing their tradition with their children.

Free. Forever. No ads.
